01

Brand

A gentle check-in.
When you need it.

Homie is a wellbeing companion that shows up quietly — phone, watch, glasses — and asks one honest question. Warm cream is the canvas, terracotta is the only thing that ever asks to be tapped.

Warm Unhurried Never clinical On your side
PRINCIPLE 01
Ask, don't assess

Every surface opens with a question, not a score. Data is a side effect of care, never the point.

PRINCIPLE 02
One thing at a time

A screen holds a single decision. If it needs a second, it needs a second screen.

PRINCIPLE 03
Soft edges, clear exits

Low-contrast, rounded surfaces — but the way out and the way to help are always obvious.

LETTERFORM RULES
Monoline. No stroke contrast anywhere in the family.
Round terminals. Every stroke ends in a full half-circle, as in the e and i.
Tall x-height. 76% of cap height — keeps small sizes friendly.
Optional arc. The wordmark's 12° arc is a lockup treatment only, never applied to running text.

07

Voice

Talk like a friend, not a form.
WE SAY
“Hey, just checking in.”
“That sounds heavy. Want to talk about it?”
“No rush — I'll be here later.”
“Thanks for telling me.”
WE DON'T
“Complete your daily wellness assessment.”
“Your mood score dropped 12% this week.”
“You've broken your 7-day streak!”
“Logged successfully.”
Short sentences
Under 12 words on a phone. Under 8 on a watch.
No diagnosis
Homie never names a condition or gives medical advice.
Always an exit
“Not now” is offered as often as “Yes”.
Crisis language
Direct, calm, no metaphors: “I can connect you with someone now.” Human help is one tap away.

Channels

Homie reaches people where they already are: a text thread on iMessage, and a voice call run by an ElevenLabs conversational agent. Neither channel gets its own app UI — the design work is the shape of the message and the shape of the conversation.

MESSAGE ANATOMY
One bubble, one idea. Never send two messages back to back; never exceed 160 characters in the opener.
Question last. The bubble ends on the question so the reply field is the obvious next move.
Quick replies are optional. Max three, verbs only, always including a low-effort out (“Just sit”).
Terracotta is the send colour. Homie's own bubbles stay system grey — the brand colour marks the person's voice and the actions, not the bot.
CADENCE & CONSENT
Max one unprompted text a day, none between 22:00 and 08:00 unless asked for.
No reply is an answer. Homie never follows up twice on the same thread.
STOP works instantly, and “pause for a week” is offered before it's needed.
First message of any thread re-states who this is and how to stop.
“Hi — it's Homie. I'll check in once a day. Reply STOP any time and I'll go quiet.”

AGENT PERSONA · ELEVENLABS
Timbre — warm, mid-low, unhurried. No upward-inflected retail brightness.
Pace — ~135 wpm, slower than the caller by a touch.
Stability 0.55 · Similarity 0.75 — enough variance to sound alive, not enough to drift.
Silence is allowed. The agent waits a full 2.5 s before filling a pause.
Barge-in always on. The caller interrupting is a feature, never an error state.
CALL SCRIPT SPINE
01“Hi, it's Homie. Is now an okay time?” — consent before content.
02One open question. Then listen.
03Reflect back in the caller's own words before adding anything.
04Offer one next step, never three.
05Close warm: “I'll check in tomorrow. You can call me first.”
ESCALATION · THE ONE THING THE AGENT NEVER IMPROVISES
Detect
Risk language triggers a scripted branch — no generative response is spoken in that state.
Say
“I want to get you to someone who can help right now. Can I connect you?” Calm, direct, no metaphors.
Hand off
Warm transfer to a human line within 20 s, and a text with the same number lands on the thread.

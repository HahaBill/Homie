01 Brand

A gentle check-in.
When you need it.

Homie is a mental-wellbeing companion that shows up quietly — on your phone, your watch, your glasses — and asks one honest question. No streaks. No pressure. Just a friend who checks in.

Warm Unhurried Never clinical On your side
Ask, don't assess

Every surface opens with a question, not a score. Data collection is a side effect of care, never the point.

One thing at a time

A screen holds a single decision. If it needs a second, it needs a second screen.

Soft edges, clear exits

Rounded, low-contrast surfaces — but the way out and the way to help are always obvious.

CLEAR SPACE & MINIMUM SIZE
Clear space on all sides = ¼ the mark's width. Minimum size: 32 px digital, 12 mm print. Below 40 px, drop the wordmark and use the face tile alone.
DON'T
— Recolour the wordmark outside Terracotta or Charcoal.
— Rotate, skew, or re-arc the letters.
— Place the mark on a photograph without the cream tile.
— Add drop shadows, outlines, or gradients.
— Swap the face for another portrait or emoji.

07 Voice
Talk like a friend, not a form.
WE SAY
“Hey, just checking in.”
“That sounds heavy. Want to talk about it?”
“No rush — I'll be here later.”
“Thanks for telling me.”
WE DON'T
“Complete your daily wellness assessment.”
“Your mood score dropped 12% this week.”
“You've broken your 7-day streak!”
“Logged successfully.”
Short sentences
Under 12 words on a phone. Under 8 on a watch.
No diagnosis
Homie never names a condition or gives medical advice.
Always an exit
“Not now” is offered as often as “Yes”.
Crisis language
Direct, calm, no metaphors: “I can connect you with someone now.” Human help is one tap away.
